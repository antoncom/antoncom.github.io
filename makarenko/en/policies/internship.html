Internship 2025 Policy in force

Full text of the policy · Team Regulations

Starting to work with our enterprise involves three stages:

  1. an introductory period;
  2. an internship under an apprenticeship contract;
  3. employment.

1. Introductory period

The introductory period takes from 2 to 3 weeks — depending on how much time you can devote and how much experience you bring.

Your goal is to get to know the subject of the work, your specialty, and answer for yourself: do I like it or not, is it for me or not?

The Plant also has a goal during this period: to understand whether long-term, mutually beneficial cooperation between us will work out.

The introductory period is not paid in money, but it is paid with the time of the experienced engineer who will accompany you.

During the introductory period each of you will complete several small assignments to get to know the work. Their results will be the basis for our joint decision on the next stage — the internship.

2. Internship

The internship has two distinguishing features: a stipend and the nature of the tasks.

The stipend consists of a fixed part (base) and a variable part (piece-rate). Its amount depends on your entry level (junior, closer to middle, or senior) and, naturally, on the rate at which you start.

The nature of the assignments at the internship stage approaches the tasks the Plant's customers set us.

The aim of this second stage is to complete several real production assignments with minimal help from the mentor.

The Plant also has a goal: to decide after what period you can be invited to sign an employment contract.

The internship lasts from 3–6 months to a year and depends mainly on your own wish and involvement.

3. Employment

Employment differs from the internship only in the form of the contract (an employment contract instead of an apprenticeship one).

By the time of employment, the level of pay should correspond to your position (middle, junior or senior).
