“HistLit Club” Programme Policy approved

Full text of the policy · Pay and Employee Programmes

Purpose

The programme broadens engineers' horizons and develops skills that are needed at work but rarely practised there: getting to grips with an unfamiliar subject, selecting sources and briefly presenting conclusions to colleagues. For a distributed team, club meetings are also a reason to talk about non-work topics.

Approach

Participation is voluntary. Participants choose a topic themselves and, in their own time, read fiction, non-fiction or history on it.

After reading, the participant prepares a short talk for colleagues.

Procedure and rewards

  • Club meetings are held no more than once a month and last 45 minutes. At a meeting the speaker presents the results of their research.
  • Each participant in a meeting is paid for 1 hour of working time.
  • At the end of the year, by participants' vote, speakers receive a 1st, 2nd or 3rd degree bonus: 15, 10 and 5 thousand roubles respectively.

Evaluation

After a year the manager reviews the results: how many meetings took place, how many employees took part and how they rate the club. Depending on the results, the programme is continued, changed or closed.
