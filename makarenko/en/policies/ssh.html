Six Sun Hours (SSH) Policy in force

Full text of the policy · Team Regulations

We work in daylight.

If you have no way of shifting your working routine to daylight hours, it is better to decline cooperation with the Plant.

If you have already worked at night but were unable to change your routine of work and rest, be prepared for the cooperation to end.

For full-time employees

Set aside at least 6 hours for work that fall in the daytime in your time zone. A weekday with not a single report in TeamTime counts as your unpaid day off (see the Daily Reports Policy).

For part-timers and interns

Make sure that the appropriate number of actual hours is logged in TeamTime for each working week. A working week without a single report in TeamTime counts as your unpaid leave.
