Monthly Work Plan Policy in force

Full text of the policy · Team Regulations

At the start of the month, at the regular “Rhythm” meeting, we discuss the list of tasks for the month.

For this discussion each employee prepares an answer to the question: are they ready to promise to complete the plan by the end of the month?

Once the amount of work for the month has been agreed in this way, your promise is as binding as paying salaries on time.

Take your words seriously.

The project manager's duty is to prepare detailed assignments for each employee by the start of the month.

What if I can't meet the deadline

Clearly, the problem may have an objective or a subjective cause.

Objective — when unrealistic deadlines were set or unforeseen technical difficulties were not taken into account from the start. There will be nothing to reproach you for if you keep your daily reports carefully, with no idle time — see the Six Sun Hours (SSH) Policy.

Subjective — personal or family circumstances. You will not be reproached if you report in good time that you cannot complete the assignments by the set deadline.
