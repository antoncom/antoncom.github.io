“Rhythm” Meetings Policy in force

Full text of the policy · Team Regulations

The “Rhythm” is intended to reduce the number of interruptions during the working week.

The team is growing, and with every chat message you will be distracting someone. The more responsible the position, the more precious that person's attention.

So each of us is asked to divide questions, before asking them, into two groups: urgent and non-urgent.

  • Non-urgent — write them down in a notebook and prepare them for the next call in the “RZE_Rhythm” chat.
  • Urgent — those related to production — before asking, ask yourself: will Google answer this for me? If “yes”, try to find the answer yourself.

If not, then before distracting a colleague with a question, prepare half the answer. Better still, think through options for a solution and ask only which one to choose.

Reports in TeamTime are a good place to formulate such options.

How the “Rhythm” is run

We propose holding the “Rhythm” once every 2 weeks, at the time set by the CEO.

The whole “Rhythm” lasts exactly 20 minutes. So prepare your questions so that the essence is clear within 1 minute. Bear in mind that the person answering will have only 1–2 minutes. Don't put each other in an awkward position: questions requiring careful consideration should be taken outside the “Rhythm”.

A secretary is appointed for each “Rhythm”. They must listen carefully and, after the “Rhythm”, write a brief summary in their report. In addition, the secretary prepares the agenda of the next “Rhythm” in advance, collecting the questions employees have prepared for the next meeting.

More on the secretary's duties is in a separate instruction (the “Rhythm” Secretary Policy).

Logging in TeamTime

On the day of the meeting a separate time-tracking task will appear in your employee account. Those who took part must log the time spent and close the task.
