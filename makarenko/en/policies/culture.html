Corporate Culture Policy: max(R) = max(D) in force

Full text of the policy · Team Regulations

This is about the culture of working relations at our enterprise. One phrase is enough to describe it:

“Maximum respect with maximum demands” (c)

Or, in programmers' language: max(R) = max(D).

max(R) means:

  1. We have people who raise the money.

    They boldly take responsibility before investors, secure social and other guarantees, move the business into special economic zones and, finally, put their own property at risk by going to the bank for a loan when there is no other way to pay employees' salaries on time. Their selfless work over many years is an expression of maximum respect for the collective and for each of its members.

  2. We have people who write the technical specifications.

    They carefully analyse customer requests to make sure the task has a technical solution. Their concern is not to demand the impossible from those who carry out the work. This is an expression of maximum respect for colleagues.

  3. We have mentors.

    Sharing their experience with newcomers, they are not afraid of losing their place, since there is always room for a step forward. Training your own replacement is an expression of maximum respect for the enterprise.

  4. We have Seniors, Middles and Juniors.

    After 1 year of work a senior can become a project manager, a middle a senior, a junior a middle. The opportunity to gain experience and climb the career ladder is an expression of maximum respect for personal ambitions.

  5. We have interns.

    We do not invite them on probation and we do not test anyone. We invite newcomers to get acquainted, give them the chance to try themselves in our work and, from the height of our experience, decide whether we can be useful to each other. This is an expression of maximum respect for the diversity of everyone's talents.

max(D) means:

  1. Six Sun Hours (SSH) Policy
  2. Daily Reports Policy
  3. “Chat Time 15:00” Policy
  4. “Rhythm” Meetings Policy
  5. Monthly Work Plan Policy
  6. Internship 2025 Policy

Summary

In all working relationships and in new situations, we are all guided by the principle “Maximum respect with maximum demands” (c).
