Salary Inflation Coefficient Policy was applied

Full text of the policy · Pay and Employee Programmes

This policy establishes automatic monthly indexation of employees' salaries to account for inflation.

Purpose

With high inflation a fixed salary loses purchasing power every month. If it is raised only at the employee's request, conflicts of interest arise that can be avoided:

  • an employee who feels awkward asking for a raise eventually starts looking for another job, and the company loses them without warning;
  • an employee who regularly secures raises spends their own and their manager's time on negotiations instead of work;
  • salaries of employees at the same level start to depend on bargaining skills rather than on results.

Indexation by a rule known in advance settles these questions. The employee knows their income is protected, and meetings with the manager are about work, not money.

Benchmark for indexation

The annual benchmark is the Bank of Russia key rate on the date the coefficient is approved. The Bank of Russia usually keeps its key rate above current inflation, so indexing by it gives the employee a margin over the official consumer price index. This margin reflects the fact that an employee's personal expenses (food, rent, services) often rise faster than the national average.

Calculation

The annual benchmark is converted into a monthly coefficient using the compound-interest formula:

Kmonth = (1 + annual rate)^(1/12)
Salary for the next month = Salary for the previous month × Kmonth

Example: at a key rate of 14.5% per year, Kmonth = 1.145^(1/12) ≈ 1.01135, i.e. a raise of 1.13% a month. Over 12 months these raises together give growth of exactly 14.5%.

Review and suspension

  • The coefficient is reviewed at least once a year, and also when the key rate changes significantly.
  • If the enterprise's financial position does not allow indexation to continue, management may suspend it. Employees are notified in advance, with the reason and duration of the suspension.
  • During the suspension the salary already reached is not reduced.
  • Once the financial position recovers, indexation resumes at the coefficient then in effect.
